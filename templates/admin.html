Academic calendar

When the term starts and ends, plus the important dates (holidays, breaks, deadlines, exams). The "Add to calendar" export on the Plan page repeats each class weekly between these dates and skips the breaks. A section's own dates from the Excel file (Start Date / End Date) are used first when they exist.

Only the admin account (the first one created) can change the academic calendar. You can see the current dates below.

Important dates

Everything from the AUPP academic calendar: holidays, breaks, add/drop and withdrawal deadlines, exam period, graduation. "Campus closed" holidays and breaks mean no classes: class meetings are skipped on those days in the calendar export. Students get all of these as all-day events (deadlines come with reminders). For a single day, leave "To" empty.

How should new data be applied?

Applies to the Excel upload and the pasted text below. Offerings are shared by everyone using this app. Use "Update" for a refreshed seat count or a few new sections, "Replace" for a brand-new term.

1

Upload the term's course schedule (Excel)

What to upload: the registrar's course schedule for the term as an Excel file (.xlsx), one row per class section, for example "Spring 2027 Undergraduate Tentative Schedule". It should have columns like course code, section, title, days and time, credits, instructor, and optionally room, seats and prerequisites. The app guesses the columns and lets you fix them in the next steps; nothing is saved until you confirm at the very end.

Not here: a student's degree audit or grade report (those go on the Curriculum page) and the academic calendar PDF (its dates are typed into the Academic calendar card above).

Which mode? A new term: choose Replace everything above. Rooms or seats updated, or another faculty's schedule added: choose Update. Currently loaded: see "Current offerings" at the bottom of this page.

2

Choose the sheet

This workbook has more than one sheet — registrars often keep past terms in the same file. Pick the one with this term's actual course list; the wrong one will look empty or fail to map.
